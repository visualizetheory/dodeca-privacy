Privacy Policy

Dodeca processes audio files and microphone recordings on your device to detect and display musical notes.

Data handling

Dodeca does not create user accounts and does not send audio recordings, imported files, analysis results, or preferences to a server. Audio and analysis results remain on your device unless you choose to remove them.

Microphone access

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record audio. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.
